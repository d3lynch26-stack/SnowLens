Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. This policy covers the SnowLens website and pilot waitlist, operated by SnowLens LLC, Maryland, USA. It does not describe a future app's video-processing practices; those will be provided before collecting footage through that service.

Information we collect and why

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address and a record of the signup notice to send pilot updates and manage your request. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you include. Please do not send skiing videos, payment details, or sensitive information through the waitlist.

Services used by this website

  • Formspree processes waitlist submissions and delivers them to us. It may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and submission time for operation and security. Its services may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and may process request logs and technical information to deliver and secure it. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used here. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve them, which exposes technical information including your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • Our email provider processes messages and waitlist notifications so we can communicate with you.

Your choices and retention

Joining is voluntary. To stop updates, request deletion, correct your email, or ask for a copy of your information, email hello@getsnowlens.com. You may withdraw your signup consent at any time. We retain waitlist information while managing the pilot and your subscription to updates, subject to removal requests and any necessary legal or security records. Service providers may retain separate technical logs under their policies. We do not promise immediate removal from every backup.

Cookies and sharing

The current landing page does not add advertising or analytics scripts or use browser storage for signup tracking. External services have their own policies. We use service providers to operate the website and waitlist; information may also need to be disclosed to comply with legal obligations.

Age and security

The waitlist is intended for adults aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us to request removal. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Questions and changes

Contact SnowLens LLC at hello@getsnowlens.com. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change.
